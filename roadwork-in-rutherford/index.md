## Tree Trimming

PSE&G has contracted with two different companies to perform tree trimming work during the month of February. 

This is routine maintenance to clear branches from power lines. The areas of focus are the majority of the West End as well as the southern portion of town from Highland Cross and Donaldson Ave to Rt 3.

[View Map Of Tree Trimming Areas Here](https://storage.googleapis.com/static.rutherford-nj.com/road-work/January_2025_TreeTrimming.pdf)

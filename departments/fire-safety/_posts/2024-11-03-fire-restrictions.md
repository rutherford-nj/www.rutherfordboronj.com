ADVISORY: Stage 3 Fire Restrictions in Effect

**Due to the ongoing drought, the NJDEP has banned fires for recreational use on all public lands and private properties in all municipalities throughout the state.** 

Visit [https://www.nj.gov/dep/parksandforests/fire/](https://www.nj.gov/dep/parksandforests/fire/) for the most up to date information. 

All fires will be prohibited unless contained in an elevated stove using only propane, natural gas, or electricity.

The following fires are strictly prohibited: 

- Wood and charcoal grill fires
  
- Any recreational fire with open flame including chimeneas and outdoor fireplaces and fire pits
  
-  Burning of leaves or other vegetation is prohibited
  
-  Bonfires and the use of all fireworks including fireworks that may be part of a holiday or religious celebration are prohibited.

Additionally, we are seeking everyone’s cooperation is conserving water during this period of drought watch.

All fires should be reported immediately by calling 9-1-1. Questions regarding the fire ban may be directed to the Bureau of Fire Safety weekdays 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

The NJDEP Press Release can be viewed at the following link: [https://dep.nj.gov/newsrel/24_0049/](https://dep.nj.gov/newsrel/24_0049/)

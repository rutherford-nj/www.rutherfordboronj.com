The Borough of Rutherford has a contract with TYCO Animal Control Services. 

Please call Tyco Animal Control for:

-	Dead animals on public property

-	Dead animals on private property (Removal fee)

-	Nuisance wildlife (Removal fee)

-	Leash law or pooper scooper violations

-	Report lost pet

TYCO Animal Control 201-652-4554

Please call the Rutherford Police Department for sick, injured, or dangerous animals, and stray domestic animals.
Police Department 201-939-6000

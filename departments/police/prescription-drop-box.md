# Prescription Medicine Drop Box 

The Rutherford Police Department has installed a Prescription Medicine Drop Box inside headquarters lobby at 18 Donaldson Avenue, which is open to the public 24/7.

We can accept the following items: Pills, capsules, patches, and pet medications in their original packaging with all personal identifiable information removed.

WE DO NOT ACCEPT ANY LIQUIDS, SYRINGES, VAPE PENS, OR ELECTRONIC CIGARETTES.
